## Description (4000 char max)
```
Clarke Mechanical puts your heating, cooling, and ventilation service in your pocket. Whether you're a customer booking a repair or a technician out on a job, everything you need is in one place.

FOR CUSTOMERS
- Request service and book an appointment at a time that works for you
- Review and approve estimates before any work begins
- View and pay invoices securely from your phone
- See your full service and equipment history
- Get appointment reminders and updates
- Message our team directly when you need support

FOR CLARKE MECHANICAL STAFF
- Manage jobs, schedules, and daily dispatch
- Build estimates, invoices, and inspection reports on site
- Track time, parts, and payments as you work
- Keep customer records and job history at your fingertips

Clarke Mechanical Inc. provides professional HVAC, heating, and ventilation service. This app connects our customers and field team so service is faster, clearer, and easier for everyone.

Questions? Contact us at service@clarkemechanicalinc.org.
```
