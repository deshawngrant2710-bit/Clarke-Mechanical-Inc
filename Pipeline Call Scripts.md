# Clarke Mechanical — Pipeline Call Scripts

Keep it short, friendly, and confident. Your goal on the first call is **not** to close a job —
it's to find the right person, get on their vendor/bid list, and set a next step.

---

## Before you dial (10 seconds)
- Glance at the lead card: company, type (Property Mgmt / GC / Owner), city, and the "Why call" note.
- Have your email ready to send info right after.
- Smile — it comes through on the phone.

---

## 1) Property Management companies

**Opening**
> "Hi, this is [Your Name] with Clarke Mechanical, a licensed New York HVAC company. Did I catch you at an okay time for a quick minute?"

*(If yes, continue. If busy: "No problem — when's a better time to reach you?" → log a callback.)*

**Reason for the call**
> "We work with property managers across the city on heating, cooling, and ventilation — service calls, repairs, replacements, and preventive maintenance. I'm reaching out to see how we can get on your approved vendor list for HVAC."

**Qualify (ask, then listen)**
- "Who handles your HVAC vendors or building maintenance — is that you or someone on your team?"
- "How many buildings/units are you managing right now?"
- "Who are you using for HVAC today, and how's that working out?"
- "Do you have anything coming up — no heat calls, rooftop units, boiler work?"

**The ask**
> "What do we need to do to get added to your vendor list? I can send our license, insurance (COI), and capabilities over today, and follow up so we're ready when something comes up."

**Close**
> "Great — I'll email that to [confirm email]. What's the best way to reach you when a job comes in?"

---

## 2) General Contractors

**Opening**
> "Hi, this is [Your Name] with Clarke Mechanical — a licensed NY HVAC company. Do you have a quick minute?"

**Reason for the call**
> "We're an HVAC subcontractor and we're looking to connect with GCs who need a dependable mechanical partner on upcoming projects — installation, replacement, repair, and maintenance."

**Qualify**
- "Who handles your subcontractor or estimating list?"
- "What kind of projects do you have coming up this year?"
- "What do you need from us to get prequalified to bid?"

**The ask**
> "I'd love to get on your bid list. What's the best email to send our prequal package — license, insurance, and past work — and who should I send it to?"

**Close**
> "Perfect. I'll get that over today and check back. When you've got a project that needs HVAC, keep us in mind for a number."

---

## 3) Building Owners / Supers (direct)

**Opening**
> "Hi, this is [Your Name] with Clarke Mechanical, a licensed NY HVAC company. Are you the right person for heating and cooling at [building/address]?"

**Reason**
> "We handle everything HVAC — no-heat emergencies, boiler and furnace repair, AC, and maintenance plans so things don't break at the worst time."

**Qualify + ask**
- "How's the system running right now — any trouble spots?"
- "Would it help to have us do a quick walkthrough / free estimate?"
> "I can get a technician out to take a look — what day works better, earlier or later in the week?"

## Voicemail (keep it under 20 seconds)
> "Hi [Name], this is [Your Name] with Clarke Mechanical, a licensed New York HVAC company. I'm reaching out about getting on your vendor list for heating and cooling work. I'll send a quick email as well — my number is [phone]. Thanks, and have a good one."

*(Then tap the amber **VM** button on the lead card — it logs the voicemail and reminds you to follow up tomorrow.)*

---

## Handling common brush-offs

- **"We already have someone."**
  > "Totally understand — most companies do. We're happy to be your backup for overflow or when they can't get there fast. Mind if I send our info so you have it when you need a second option?"

- **"Send me an email."**
  > "Will do — what's the best address? And so I send the right thing, are you more interested in service/repairs or larger install work?" *(Gets the email + qualifies.)*

- **"Not interested."**
  > "No problem at all. Can I leave you my number in case a no-heat call ever comes up and your usual guy's tied up?"

- **"How much do you charge?"**
  > "Fair question — it depends on the job. That's exactly why I'd love to do a free estimate so you get a real number, no obligation."

- **Gatekeeper: "What's this regarding?"**
  > "I'm with Clarke Mechanical, the HVAC company — just trying to reach whoever handles heating and cooling vendors. Who would that be?"

---

## After every call — log it in the pipeline
On the lead card, tap it and **Log call** with the outcome:
- **Reached — interested** → move to *Contacted* or *Quoted*, set a follow-up date.
- **Left voicemail** → tap **VM** (auto follow-up tomorrow).
- **Not interested** → move to *Lost* with a one-line reason.
- Always drop a quick **note**: who you spoke to, email captured, and the next step.

**Two things that win this game:** (1) always capture the decision-maker's name + email, and (2) always set a next follow-up. Consistent follow-up is what turns these into jobs.
