## Information we collect

**Information you provide to us:**
- Contact and account details — your name, business name, email address, phone number, and login credentials.
- Service information — service address, appointment details, the equipment and problem you describe, service and repair history, photos you or our technicians add for a job, and any notes.
- Payment information — when you pay online, your card details are entered directly with our payment processor. We do **not** collect or store full card numbers, expiration dates, or security codes on our systems. We may retain limited, non-sensitive details such as the card brand and last four digits, and a secure token, to let you reuse a saved card.
- Communications — messages, requests, and support conversations you send us.

**Information we collect automatically:**
- Basic device, app, and usage information needed to operate the Service (for example, log data and general diagnostics).
- We use only the cookies and local storage necessary to keep you signed in and to make the Service function.

## How we use your information

We use your information to:
- Schedule, dispatch, and perform HVAC and boiler service.
- Create and send appointment confirmations, reminders, estimates, invoices, and receipts.
- Process payments and manage saved payment methods.
- Communicate with you about your service, respond to requests, and provide support.
- Operate, maintain, secure, and improve the Service.
- Comply with legal, accounting, and tax obligations.

We do **not** sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

## Text messages (SMS)

If you provide your mobile number and opt in, we may send you service-related text messages — such as appointment confirmations, reminders, "on the way" notifications, and invoice or estimate notices — through our messaging provider. Message and data rates may apply, and message frequency varies.

You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying **STOP** to any message, or by updating your notification preferences in the app or contacting us. Replying **HELP** provides assistance. Opting out of texts does not remove you from essential account or billing communications sent by other means.

## How we share your information

We share information only as needed to operate our business:
- **Payment processing** — Helcim, our payment processor, to securely process card payments. Helcim handles card data under its own privacy and PCI-DSS obligations.
- **Communications providers** — our phone and messaging provider (to place calls and send texts) and our email provider (to deliver confirmations, reminders, and receipts).
- **Technology providers** — hosting, database, mapping/address, and infrastructure providers that help us run the Service.
- **Legal and safety** — we may disclose information if required by law, to enforce our agreements, or to protect the rights, safety, and property of our customers, our business, or others.

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

## How we protect your information

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your information, including encryption of data in transit and restricting access to those who need it. Online card payments are handled through a PCI-compliant payment processor. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to keep your information protected.

## Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service, and afterward as required to meet accounting, tax, warranty, and legal obligations. When information is no longer needed, we take steps to delete or de-identify it.

## Your choices and rights

- **Update your information** — you can update your contact details and notification preferences at any time in the app.
- **Delete your account** — you can delete your account from **My Portal → My Info → Delete my account**, which removes your login and portal access. We may retain certain service and billing records where required by law.
- **Access or deletion requests** — you may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, subject to legal retention requirements.
- **Marketing and texts** — you can opt out of text messages by replying STOP, and manage notification preferences in the app.

To exercise any of these choices, contact us at service@clarkemechanicalinc.org or (516) 206-6256.
